Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Mehdi Technologies ("we", "us", "our") built the Tic Tac Toe app ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

The App asks you to enter a name so it can show it on the game screen. This name, your game results, and your match history are kept only in your device's memory while the App is open. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you close the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera data.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Permissions

The App does not request any special permissions on your device.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The App works fully offline. It does not connect to the internet and does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages because no data is collected or shared.

6. Data Security

Because your information stays on your device and is not transmitted or stored by us, there is no personal data on our servers to be accessed or leaked.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version of the App collects any data, we will update this policy before release.
